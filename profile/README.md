# erlic AI

### Turkish Language AI Research & Development

**erlic AI** is an independent artificial intelligence research initiative focused on Turkish language modeling, efficient neural architectures, and advanced language model research.

Our work explores efficient model architectures and methods for improving Turkish language understanding, generation, and reasoning.

---

## Research Areas

- **Turkish Language Models:** Language modeling for Turkish and morphologically rich languages.
- **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE):** Sparse architectures and expert-based computation.
- **Natural Language Processing:** Turkish text understanding and generation.
- **Reasoning:** Mathematical, logical, and verifiable reasoning methods.
- **Efficient Training:** Memory-efficient and scalable model training.
- **Data Engineering:** Data preparation, filtering, and quality control.

## Current Project Status

erlic AI is currently in the engineering validation and compute preparation phase.

The model architecture and training pipeline are under development and validation.

Large-scale model training has not yet been completed. No trained model weights or benchmark results are currently being claimed.
